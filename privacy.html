Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

This policy describes how LexiGlade handles information. No app account is required to play.

Local game data

The app stores level progress, coins, hints, settings, and related game state locally on your device to restore your game. Removing the app can delete this local data. Device backups are managed under your Apple settings.

Advertising and consent

LexiGlade uses Google AdMob for banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded advertisements, and Google's User Messaging Platform to manage advertising consent where applicable. Advertising services may process IP addresses, device and app identifiers, device information, approximate location inferred from IP, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver and measure advertisements, prevent fraud, and maintain their services.

On iOS, the app requests App Tracking Transparency permission before accessing the advertising identifier for tracking. You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Denying tracking does not prevent ordinary gameplay. Advertising behavior also depends on your consent choices and the applicable regional requirements.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.

Analytics and diagnostics

The app includes Firebase Analytics and initializes Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to understand app usage, identify crashes, and improve reliability. These services may process app interactions and usage events, crash reports, stack traces, app and operating system versions, device information, installation identifiers, and diagnostic logs. See Firebase Privacy and Security.

Notifications and device controls

If notification permission has been granted, the app may schedule game reminders. You can manage notification permission in iOS Settings. Music, sound, and vibration preferences are stored locally. The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise GPS location for ordinary gameplay.

Support

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide, and use it to respond to your request. Please do not send sensitive information.

Retention and choices

Local game data remains until removed from your device. Third-party services retain information according to their policies and settings. You may contact us with access or deletion requests concerning information you provided directly; third-party requests may also need to be directed to the relevant provider.

Children

LexiGlade is not designed specifically for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us so we can address it.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes; the effective date will be updated here. For privacy questions, email shenhualxt1122@gmail.com or visit Support.
